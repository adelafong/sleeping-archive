Memory

Sleeping Archive explores how traditional cultural knowledge can be reinterpreted and carried forward through contemporary forms.

Inspired by Borneo's weaving traditions, the publication looks beyond the interactive installation to document weaving practices, motifs, meanings, histories and material traditions across communities including Iban, Lun Bawang, Kelabit, Bidayuh, Penan, Kenyah, Kayan, Melanau Rajang and Sarawak Malay.

Research principleMotifs are not treated as fixed symbols. Where interpretations vary, or research is limited, the archive identifies those gaps instead of filling them with assumptions.
